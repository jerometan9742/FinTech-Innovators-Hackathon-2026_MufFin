# FinTech-Innovators-Hackathon-2026
FinNex

Investors today manage wealth across fragmented ecosystems — juggling multiple banking apps, investment platforms, and crypto wallets with no unified view of their true financial health. This fragmentation obscures informed decision-making and leaves wealth vulnerabilities undetected.
FinNex is an integrated Wealth Wellness Hub that solves this by aggregating traditional assets (bank deposits, CPF, investment portfolios), private holdings, and digital assets into a single, secure Wealth Wallet. Built in response to Schroders' problem statement, FinNex delivers a complete, actionable view of an investor's total financial health in one dashboard.

At its core, FinNex quantifies financial wellness through five key metrics — Debt-to-Income Ratio, Savings Rate, Portfolio Diversification, Liquidity Ratio, and Net Worth Growth — generating a real-time Financial Health Score. The platform visualizes wealth composition, tracks health indicators over time, and surfaces personalized, data-driven recommendations tailored to each user's financial position.

A built-in Scenario Simulator allows users to stress-test their portfolios against real-world events such as market crashes or job loss, empowering proactive financial decisions. FinNex transforms fragmented financial data into clarity, confidence, and control.
